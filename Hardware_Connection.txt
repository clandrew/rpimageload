The following are the connections from the WaveShare LCD 1.69inch module to the Raspberry Pi Pico 2W.
The listed wire colors are the colors on my particular cabling unit, for illustrative purposes only. They may be different on your unit.

WaveShare LCD	Raspberry Pi Pico 2W
-------------	--------------------------
VCC	(purple)  |	3v3				Pin 39
GND (white)	  |	GND				Pin 38
DIN (green)	  |	GP19, SPI0 TX		        Pin 25
CLK (orange)      |	GP18, SPI0 SCK		        Pin 24
CS (yellow)	  |	GP17, SPI0 CSn		        Pin 22
DC (blue)	  |	GP22				Pin 29
RST (brown)       |	GP13				Pin 17	(also SPI1CSn)
BL (gray)	  |	GPU21				Pin 27

Button (red)      |     GP14                            Pin 19
Button (black)    |     GND                             Pin 18
